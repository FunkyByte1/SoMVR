Build 29b - what to test
- Everything new since build 20c, by topic. Menu > Controls lists every move.
- Smooth: the headset runs at 80 Hz with a VR graphics preset. Fights, fire and rain should stay smooth. Try Settings > Refresh rate 90; back to 80 if it stutters.
- Fire and the wraith sky look the same in both eyes, at their depth - no smoke in your face.
- Low health: only a faint red glow at the edges. No lens flare.
- Popups, XP and subtitles sit just under your gaze.

- FIGHTING. Stealth kill: sneak up, stab hard point first into his back or neck. The sword sticks; pull it out. A slow push only makes him turn.
- Grab: your empty left hand at an orc's neck - a blue glow and a tick mean squeeze. Then A: trigger = Intel, B = Dismiss, Y = Command (captains).
- Big fights: an orc behind you warns before he swings. Shield orcs attack, no endless block.
- Vault over an orc: look at him, push the right stick forward, let go. Cut his back within 2 s.
- Last chance: when an orc is about to kill you, steer the red ring's cursor in with your hand, then stab.
- Wraith flash: when the sword glows blue, hold the right trigger and push your open left palm at an orc.
- A downed orc: stab straight down into him while the sword glows.
- Drop kill: crouch on a wall top above an unaware orc (a blue glow on him), stab hard down at him.
- Brutal kill: crouch behind an unaware orc, hold the left trigger and stab his back hard. Blood stops a hand's width from your eyes.
- Captains: Practice > Captain. Fast real cuts bite: about a dozen and an execution (more if he has killed you - he comes back stronger). Immune to stealth: the blade glances off. His rune drops (B).
- Captains in the world: start a power struggle at its marker - the intro plays where he stands.
- A warchief (Piercing Light): shoot his marked supporters in the head. After five he comes with his bodyguards; at his Last Words press X and stab him quickly.
- A struggle's result: A = View Battle Report (the board), B = back.
- A beaten captain held by the game: X = Kill, then stab his chest within 4 s or he gets up. Or LT = Dominate, then LT = Gain Intel: the left stick to the warchiefs, A.

- BOW AND WRAITH. The sight is small and white, blue on an orc. Shadow Strike lands you with the sword in hand.
- One arrow in an orc's head kills him (captains keep their own rules).
- Fire arrow: bow drawn, press B - the tip glows. He burns.
- Whistle: crouch, an empty hand at your mouth a moment - an orc comes to look.
- Distract (once bought): bow drawn, press X - orcs go to look where you aim.
- Branded orcs: raise your left hand above your head and pull the left trigger.

- MOVING. Jump: A. Jump at a wall and grab its top as you rise.
- Vault: walk at a low wall (the ruin by the campfire); a gold ring shows: A.
- Forge tower: climb it by hand. At the top hold the platform's edge; the gold ring: A onto it.
- Whenever a gold ring shows (a top, a lip overhead), A takes you there.
- A double buzz at a ledge overhead: no way up there - move along. At a corner, a fling still lifts you.
- Climb down: reach down over a wall top's or roof's edge (even right at it) and grip - you hang; climb down.
- Drop: hold A over a drop; the white ring empties; let go at the flash.

- BEASTS. A wild caragor's leap (or Big Game's): hold your sword across its line - it's stunned. Grip it with your left hand and keep gripping through "Hold tight!". Practice > Caragor's leap can't be countered.
- Riding: the stick steers like a wheel. Tap A = leap, hold A or click the left stick = sprint, right trigger bite, B off. The bow works from the saddle. Retry keeps you on.
- Big Game's runner: sprint the caragor into him, then bite.

- STORY. Queen of the Shore: after "Ability Unlocked: Brand", crouch behind an archer, open left hand on his head, left trigger. Six unseen.
- The Rescue: lift Lithariel with your left grip. If she drops, cut the Uruks off her and pick her up.
- Mission Failed: A = Retry (even while gripping), B = Abandon. Nemesis pages: B closes.
- Cards that pause the game show their text; A closes them.

- MENUS. Map: click the right stick. Point and pull to slide a place to the cross; pull on the cross = a waypoint, shown in the world. Hold the click = Sauron's Army.
- Dying: you stay in your own eyes; point at a captain on the big screen.
- The arrival card: A closes it (no jump). Settings > Walk toward: Head or Hand.
- Bug notes: talk up to 30 s. A session lasts up to 4 hours.
