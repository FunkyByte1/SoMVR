Build 40 (Windows) - what to test
- New in 40 first (from your 11-05 notes), then 39's list. Menu > Controls lists every move.
- STRUGGLES (41): a caragor pins you - shove or stab at it again and again, a thump for each that counts; the white ring runs down to free you.
- ON YOUR KNEES or at a captain's finisher: a blue blade shows on him. Lay your sword on it, then thrust.
- Story moments: a ring = keep a hand closed until it runs out; an arrow = swipe or lean that way.
- SNEAK UP: duck and walk slowly behind an orc that hasn't seen you: he never hears you, and you get to about half a metre. Grab his shoulder, push the knife in. Walking or standing he hears you as before.
- BLOCK ZONE: an orange zone shows where each blow will land. Put your blade in it: it turns blue and you block.
- B + Y: just press both, no hold. If no answer comes within 40 s you hear "No live answer right now".
- CLIMB DOWN faster (Settings > Climbing > Climb down sets the speed). Grab during the lowering to take over. Hold A at an edge climbs down whichever way you face; stick back + A = the ring to drop all the way.
- ON A WALL: shove hard away and let go to throw yourself off. Double-tap A to let go. Walk onto beams and copings.
- FINISHER TEST: Practice > Finisher test: an orc ready in front, numbers after each try, re-armed by itself.
- HERBS: a white outline around the plant; reach over it and grip, even high up.
- GRASS AND LEAVES vanish within about 75 cm around you, feet included.
- Patches made before you launch are already in at the start (Undo on the Test tab).

Build 39 - new then:
- PINNED MENU: hold the left menu button ~half a second: the menu floats in front of you; point with either hand. "Back to the wrist" or "X Close" at the bottom.
- CLIMB DOWN: at an edge, face away and hold the stick back + A: you are lowered onto the wall under the lip. A alone steps you off. The lip glows with "A DOWN".
- CLIMB UP: on foot, rest a hand on a ledge top: a ring with "A UP"; press A to get up.
- ON A WALL: stick left/right slides you along it and round corners. No more floating stuck, no clipping into rock at corners, rough walls and longer reach.
- FINISHER: a glowing blue blade, starting within a foot of your body. Lay your sword on it (clank + buzz), thrust in at your speed: he dies at once. Pull out freely.
- FINISHER TEST: Practice > Finisher test - an orc stands in front, the blade ready. Try a grip; the numbers over him show the snap, your speed in, the depth and the kill. It re-arms by itself; B ends it.
- BLOCKS: a blocked orc reels back at once (no freeze).
- THE GAME'S OWN triangles over orcs are back, on the orc as he notices, searches and attacks.
- HERBS: Practice > New herb takes you to one. Reach: a ring tightens; grip and bring it to your mouth: buzz + green flash.
- BOW: the arrow lies beside the grip, not through it. On a wall, let go with both hands, take the bow and shoot from there.
- SOUND: the fire on your sheathed sword no longer swings ear to ear. RAIN AND FOG: half as much. SHRUBS vanish within about 2 feet of your eyes.

- LIVE NOTES. Press B + Y and say what you see; we get the note about a second after you stop. Keep playing.
- You hear "Got it" with a buzz in the left hand when you stop talking. The answer is spoken, and its card pops up by itself beside your left hand at a calm moment (8 s, or tap it away). A long answer comes as cards 1 of 2, 2 of 2. An orange dot on the back of your left hand = a card waits.
- THE MENU stays open while your other hand points at it. Turn the palm down to close it. Refresh rate: Settings > Display, last row (90 Hz is set).
- A fix comes as "Patch N ready": Menu > Test tab > Apply patch swaps it in within seconds, no restart, at a calm moment (not mid-fight or mid-climb). Your view should not move at all. Undo patch puts the one before back.
- Play as usual while a fix is being built in the background; it should feel exactly the same. If you notice any stutter, say 'stutter' in a mark.
- PRACTICE TRIP: it always lands you on solid ground now. From far away the screen stays black a few seconds and you are back where you were: get closer and try again.
- ROUGH WALLS: the two rock walls you got stuck on - pull from chest to waist: you go up past the bumps, no false 'no way up' buzz.

- KILLS. Every kill shows at once: a hard cut or stab kills him on the spot or he visibly starts falling right away - never a collapse later or a walk-off. A hard cut across his neck takes his head off.
- Hit an orc while he is dying: blood and a hit buzz. Cut an unaware orc's back: he fights you (a stab from behind still kills silently).
- Practice > Charge execution: if no orc is near, one appears facing you; face him: the finisher's blue outline. Sword glowing but no outline? A blue ring on the nearest orc says FACE TO FACE / IN REACH.
- Block his blow, then kill him on the counter: the game's counter-kill fall. A stunned orc has its own.
- FINISHER. When the sword glows, face an orc up close: the world slows and a glowing blue blade points into his neck. Bring your sword onto it: a clank, a buzz and a flash, and your blade pulls onto the line. Thrust in: he dies the moment it is in. Pull back at your own speed: the blade comes out with your hand.
- Wait or turn away: the outline goes and the world speeds up again.
- NO PROMPTS. No gold Y when an orc winds up, no button prompts at the bottom, no icons over fire pits or herbs (our own glows stay). Mark any you still see.
- Stealth: crouch behind an unaware orc and stab his back hard: he dies silently on your blade. Nothing moves you or your view.

- HITS BY FORCE. Rest or tap the blade on an orc, or flick your wrist at him: nothing. A full arm swing lands; a hard one lands heavy.
- Block his low lunge at your belly: his blow stalls at your blade for a moment.
- Crouch over a knocked-down orc and cut him - he can be hurt while down.

- THE KNIFE. Reach either hand to its own hip until it ticks, then grip: the knife is in that hand while you hold the grip; open the hand and it is back on the belt. (Right hand: when it holds nothing.)
- KNIFE KILL. Knife out, crouch behind or beside an unaware orc. Your free hand can grip his shoulder or neck: he holds still and quiet. Push the point slowly into his neck or back: he dies at once, silently. The knife sticks - pull it out.
- The knife on an orc who is fighting you is only a light hit. A brush along his back does nothing.

- THE SWORD has weight now. A quick flick leaves the blade behind; a full committed swing carries it.
- Cut an orc across the chest: the blade bites in and drags out. A slow cut stops in him.
- Stab his chest and hold: the blade stays in him and moves with him. Pull straight back to draw it out.

- YOUR FREE HAND. Shove an orc hard (a hard push, from a little further away now): he staggers 2-3 m back. With the sword out, reach and close your hand: a grab, not a shove.
- Grab: a faint blue glow on an orc's collar shows where - reach for it and make a fist. Hold him up to 10 s.
- Holding him: any sword hit kills him, a push lets him go, pull him to your face to question him. B + Y (a note) never kills him.

- THE WATCH. Look at your left wrist as if reading a watch: the map shows on your forearm and your whole hand stays visible. Turn the wrist away and it's gone.
- HERBS. Walk up to a blue healing flower until its prompt shows, reach down and grip it, then bring your fist to your mouth: you eat it and heal. (B still works.)
- LOW HEALTH: a red, pulsing ring around your view and a heartbeat in your hands. Try Practice > Refill health after taking hits.
- Left palm menu opens mid-fight now (just after a swing).
- BOW. Draw to the cheek: the string near your face fades away, and the bow no longer flickers when you twist your wrist.
- With the bow drawn, tap X: nothing. Hold X half a second: the Shadow Strike.
- Fight page > 'Bow from: Grip': squeeze the left grip anywhere to take the bow out; let go to put it away.

- CLIMBING. Pull a hold up hard and let go: you slide on down the wall. Grab to stop, or land at the bottom.
- Hanging by one hand, reach over your free shoulder for the sword and cut an orc.
- At a ledge, reach your open hand out over the edge at chest height (a light tick), then squeeze: you swing down and hang from the lip.
- Walk along the long wall's top: small sloped lips step you over by themselves. One spot just past the first lip still stops you - jump with A.
- Walk off any edge: you fall within half a second - never walk on air. At a low ledge that holds you: push toward it until A double-pulses, then A takes you down.
- Climbing, the pull-up ring shown: A takes you up, even right after letting go.

- NOTHING IN YOUR FACE. Walk or crouch into a bush and lean in: leaves within about a foot of your eyes melt away and come back as you pull out. Small flies and mosquitoes stay about an arm's length away.

- GRASS. In the meadow's tall grass, look at the tufts near you: rounder, the front blades nearer than the back. Look down and tilt your head: the grass keeps its shape.

- WEATHER. The meadow opens on a clear day (sun shadows): walls, paving and grass show their relief. If it turns grey and foggy later, that is the game's weather cycle - say if it bothers you.
- LOOKS. On the cliff over the misty valley, turn and tilt your head: the fog and mist should stay put, no wall following you.
- Look at the sun and tilt your head: its rays stay still, no dimming as you look around.

- PRACTICE > Place orc: aim your sword hand at the ground (a gold ring, even below a ledge), close the panel, pull the trigger: an unaware orc there. Try the drop kill.
- The Test page remembers its page.

- BUZZES. A light cut, a heavy blow and a kill should feel clearly different; a block cracks and rings. Every "no" is the same two soft clicks. A herb gives three bites.

- GRAUG (Torvin's hunt). At "Shoot it in the head!", draw the bow fully, hold about a second, aim at its head and let go: it is stunned.

- WRAITH HANDS. Hold Y for the wraith vision and look at your hands: pale blue, see-through, glowing edges.
- BOW. Squeeze the trigger and A together while drawing: no jump.
- Look along the bow at rest and drawn: the string starts and ends where each limb meets its glowing spike.

- SETTINGS. Menu > Settings now has groups (Comfort, Display, Climbing, Sword, Fight, Bow) under the tabs. Try Force needed Low if you play seated, and the Watch and Shove push rows.

- NOT REACHED IN BUILD 30. Last chance: when an orc is about to kill you, steer the red ring's cursor in with your hand, then stab.
- Wraith flash: when the sword glows blue, hold the right trigger and push your open left palm at an orc.
- A downed orc: stab straight down into him while the sword glows.
- Drop kill: crouch on a wall top above an unaware orc (a blue glow on him), stab hard down.
- Brutal kill: crouch behind an unaware orc, hold the left trigger and stab his back hard.
- Captains: Practice > Captain. Fast real cuts bite; his Last Words: X, then stab his chest.
- Fire arrow: bow drawn, press B. Whistle: crouch, an empty hand at your mouth.
- Caragor: Practice > Caragor. Block the leap with the sword across, walk to the stunned beast, grip it when it glows; keep gripping to ride. B gets off.
- Map: click the right stick; point and pull. Hold the click = Sauron's Army.
- Bug notes: talk up to 30 s; each one now keeps a picture of what you saw. A session lasts up to 4 hours.
