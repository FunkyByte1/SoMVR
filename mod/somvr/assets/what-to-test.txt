Build 21 - what to test
- Stealth kill: sneak behind an orc and stab hard, point first, deep into his back or neck. A touch or a slow push only makes him turn.
- After the stab the sword stays in him as he slumps. Pull it out. Blood should come from the wound.
- Grab: in a fight, reach your empty left hand to an orc's neck. A faint blue glow and a tick mean squeeze to grab. No glow at a raised shield.
- Shield orc: he should attack you, not block forever.
- Shadow Strike with the sword on your back: you land with the sword in your hand.
- Drop down: hold A over a drop. The white ring empties; at the flash and tick, let go to drop. Let go early and nothing happens.
- Climb down: grab lower on the wall and push that hand up.
- The wall by the meadow ladder: a ring shows on the real top; pull up or press A.
- Bow sight: small, thin, white; blue on an orc.
- XP: kill an orc while looking to the side. The XP shows just under your gaze and stays put.
- The low sun: no lens flare, no white rays.
- Bug notes: talk as long as you need (up to 30 s).
- Not fixed yet: fire looks different in each eye; the wraith world's fog and the interrogation smoke.
