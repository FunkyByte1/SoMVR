Build 25 - what to test
- New since build 20c, most important first: build 25's own, then 24's, then 21-23's.
- Mission Failed (you died in a mission): A = Retry, B = Abandon.
- Nemesis pages after a fight ("Power Increased!", a captain's fate): B closes them.
- Branded orcs: raise your left hand above your head and pull the left trigger - they attack.
- Drop kill: crouch on a wall top above an unaware orc. A faint blue glow on his back means it's there. Sword drawn, stab hard down at him: a short black, then you're behind him as he dies. A slow lowering does nothing.
- Captains in the world: start a power struggle at its map marker. His intro plays where he stands; his name and line show at your gaze.
- A captain immune to stealth: stab his back and the blade glances off - a clank, sparks, he turns on you.
- Artifacts: hold Y, B picks one up; the right stick turns it; B backs out.
- Brand: Queen of the Shore, at the Sea Fort. Close "Ability Unlocked: Brand" first. Crouch and walk up behind an archer until you stop, put your open left hand on his head, pull the left trigger. Brand six unseen to go on.
- Captain fight: Practice > Captain. Fast, real cuts take big bites; taps barely scratch him. About a dozen good cuts and an execution.
- After a captain's kill: Pause > Sauron's Army shows his body in his slot; his rune lies by him (B).
- Low health: no blood drops or red wash in your eyes, only a faint red glow at the edges.
- Vault: in the ruin by the campfire, walk at a low wall or block. A gold ring shows past it or on top: press A.
- Climb down: on a wall top, reach down over the edge and grip. You glide over and hang; climb down or hold A to drop.
- Jump at a wall and grab its top as you rise: the climb catches you.
- Fire: by a campfire or torch, close one eye, then the other. The flame should look the same in both and sit at its depth.
- Wraith vision: hold Y and look up. The clouds should be one calm sky far away, not smoke in your face.
- Stealth kill: sneak behind an orc and stab hard, point first, deep into his back or neck. A touch or a slow push only makes him turn. The sword sticks as he slumps; pull it out.
- Walk up with the sword held out: touching his back that way doesn't wake him.
- Grab: in a fight, reach your empty left hand to an orc's neck. A faint blue glow and a tick mean squeeze.
- Captain: Practice > Captain. His intro plays where he stands; his name and first line show together under him. A fresh captain has no grab glow: wear him down to about a third and it appears. He can kill you.
- Beasts: Practice > Caragor or Graug. Cut a caragor's head and back; hold the sword across between you and its head when it lunges. A pounce never moves your view.
- Shield orc: he attacks, no endless block.
- Shadow Strike: you land with the sword in your hand.
- Jump: press A while moving.
- Drop: hold A over a drop. The white ring empties; at the flash and tick, let go.
- Overhang: hold its edge from below. If a gold ring shows on top, press A.
- Bow sight: small and white; blue on an orc.
- XP and subtitles show just under your gaze and stay put.
- Dying: you stay in your own eyes as the world greys. On the big screen, point at a captain and press.
- Pause menu: the board is bigger. Buy a skill and equip a rune with the laser or a fingertip.
- The low sun: no lens flare, no white rays.
- Bug notes: talk as long as you need (up to 30 s).
